🥔🌿 Aloo Drumstick Curry

A spicy, comforting curry with a mustard-ginger-garlic masala.

🌶️ Masala Paste

Grind together rai (mustard seeds), garlic, chilli and ginger with a little water.

🛒 Curry Ingredients

  • Oil
  • Rai
  • Jeera
  • Curry leaves
  • 4–5 tomatoes, made into paste
  • Coriander powder
  • Chilli powder
  • Haldi
  • Salt
  • Aloo
  • Drumsticks
  • Fresh coriander

👩🏻‍🍳 Method

  1. Heat oil in the pressure cooker. Add rai, jeera and curry leaves and let them splutter.
  2. Add the ground ginger-garlic-chilli-mustard paste.
  3. Cook until the raw smell fades.
  4. Add tomato paste, coriander powder, chilli powder, haldi and salt. Cook the masala well.
  5. Add potatoes and cook for about 5 minutes.
  6. Add drumsticks and enough water for the curry.
  7. Close the pressure cooker and cook for 1 whistle.
  8. Once pressure releases, finish with chopped fresh coriander.

⭐ Naheed's Beginner Note

Don't rush the masala! The raw smell should disappear before you move to the next step. 🌿
